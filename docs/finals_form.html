How to read this

Model and sources

The guide uses all 168 completed league matches through Round 24, current points/goal difference, season attack and defence, home/away splits, and a light recent-five weighting. Fixture score probabilities come from a regularised Poisson model. Top-four and finishing probabilities come from 200,000 seeded simulations of the remaining nine fixtures involving the contenders, ranking teams by points, goal difference and goals scored. Projected points are the modal whole-number total, not an average. A displayed probability above 99.9% is modelled near-certainty, not mathematical qualification. Estimates are analytical—not official odds—and team availability, suspensions and late fixture changes are not included.

Sources: Football NSW Dribl ladder, fixtures and results, accessed 17 August 2026.
